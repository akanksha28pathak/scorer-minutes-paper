   * Why a judge marks it down: Rebuilding Table 4.1 exposed two dramatic anomalies that the prose never mentions:

A judge in agentic systems will immediately spot those two rows and ask: Why did 4 sessions on A100 run at half the speed of 5 sessions, and why does "2 attempts per task" cut per-session tokens/s by 31%? Leaving them unexplained undermines Reading (1) and Reading (3).
   * Concrete fix: Add one sentence in Section 4.1 explaining both rows (e.g., whether `"2 attempts per task"` runs 2 sub-agents concurrently inside each of the 5 sessions—effectively Concurrency 10—or busts the prefix cache, and why the 1-run `A100 @ 4 sessions` was throttled/MIG-sliced/unquantized).
